What is Artificial Intelligence?

Let's understand AI in the simplest possible way.

🤖

Think of AI as a smart problem solver.

Artificial intelligence is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require human-like abilities such as recognizing patterns, understanding language and making predictions.

💡 Real-world example

When a music app recommends a song you might enjoy, it can use patterns from your previous activity to make a prediction.
